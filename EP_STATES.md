# Emotional Physics — State Model  
**EP by ACL**

Emotional states are not personality traits.  
They are **field conditions** produced by the interaction between safety, energy, and perception.

This document defines emotional states as **measurable phase behaviors** inside human systems.

---

## 1. The State Principle

In Emotional Physics, an emotional state is defined as:

> **A temporary configuration of energy, perception, and regulation inside a safety field.**

States are not identities.  
They are **weather patterns** inside the nervous system.

---

## 2. Primary Emotional States

All human emotional experience can be modeled using a small set of field states.

These states emerge when safety changes.

---

### **Calm**

- High safety  
- High regulation  
- High coherence  
- Energy flows smoothly  

Calm is not boredom.  
It is **available capacity**.

---

### **Joy**

- High safety  
- High energy  
- High coherence  

Joy is energy in motion without threat.

---

### **Love**

- High safety  
- High connection  
- High trust  

Love is **shared coherence**.

---

### **Anxiety**

- Threat detected  
- Energy rises  
- Perception narrows  

Anxiety is **energy preparing to respond**.

It becomes pathological only when threat never resolves.

---

### **Fear**

- Threat confirmed  
- Energy spikes  
- Escape or defense activated  

Fear is **accurate when danger exists**.  
It becomes trauma when danger becomes permanent.

---

### **Anger**

- Boundary violation detected  
- Energy mobilized  

Anger is **protective force**.

It becomes destructive when safety cannot be restored.

---

### **Shame**

- Social threat detected  
- Energy collapses inward  
- Visibility feels dangerous  

Shame is **defensive withdrawal**.

It destroys coherence when chronic.

---

### **Grief**

- Loss detected  
- Energy releases attachment  

Grief is **reorganization after loss**.

It is painful because it is healing.

---

### **Dissociation**

- Overload detected  
- Energy disconnects from perception  

Dissociation is **emergency shutdown**.

It protects the system from overwhelming threat.

---

### **Depression**

- Chronic low safety  
- Energy suppressed  
- Motivation collapses  

Depression is not laziness.  
It is **energy conservation under threat**.

---

## 3. State Transitions

States are not fixed.

They shift when:
- Safety increases
- Threat resolves
- Connection returns
- Boundaries are restored

This is why:
- Love can follow anger  
- Calm can follow fear  
- Joy can return after grief  

The field reorganizes when safety returns.

---

## 4. Why Labeling People Is Wrong

Calling someone:
- “Anxious”
- “Angry”
- “Depressed”

is a category error.

You are naming a **state**, not a person.

EP replaces blame with **field awareness**.
