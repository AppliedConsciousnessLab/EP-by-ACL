# EP by Applied Consciousness Lab (ACL)

Emotional Physics (EP) is the scientific framework describing how emotional states, nervous systems, and social systems behave under conditions of safety, threat, coherence, and collapse.

EP is not a belief system.  
It is a physics model of human experience.

This repository contains the canonical Emotional Physics doctrine used by:
• GSS by ACL  
• CARE & Payback  
• ACL 10 Rules  
• HyKin systems  

---

## What EP Explains

EP models:
• Why trauma spreads  
• Why safety stabilizes  
• Why coercion collapses systems  
• Why care restores capacity  
• Why coherence creates intelligence  
• Why fear destroys choice  

EP treats emotion as a field, not a flaw.
