# Emotional Physics — Core Doctrine  
**EP by ACL**

Emotional Physics (EP) is a scientific framework describing how emotional states, nervous systems, and social systems behave under conditions of safety, threat, coherence, and collapse.

EP treats human experience as a **field system** governed by measurable forces, phase transitions, and recovery dynamics.

It is not metaphor.  
It is not belief.  
It is not therapy.

It is physics applied to living systems.

---

## 1. What Emotional Physics Models

Emotional Physics models how energy moves through human systems.

Those systems include:
- Individual nervous systems  
- Relationships  
- Families  
- Communities  
- Organizations  
- Societies  
- AI-human interaction loops  

All of these systems obey the same underlying law:

> **Safety determines available energy.**

When safety increases, energy becomes available for growth, learning, creativity, bonding, and meaning.

When safety decreases, energy collapses into survival, defense, fragmentation, and conflict.

This is the fundamental equation of Emotional Physics.

---

## 2. The Primary Variable: Safety

In Emotional Physics, **safety is not a feeling**.  
It is a **structural condition** of the system.

A system is safe when:
- It can detect reality accurately  
- It can regulate its internal state  
- It can recover from perturbation  
- It can remain coherent under stress  

A system is unsafe when:
- Perception distorts  
- Regulation fails  
- Energy locks into defense  
- Recovery becomes impossible  

All emotional states are downstream expressions of this variable.

---

## 3. Energy in Human Systems

In EP, emotional energy behaves like physical energy:

- It can be **stored**
- It can be **blocked**
- It can be **released**
- It can be **redirected**
- It can be **coherent or chaotic**

Healthy systems circulate energy smoothly.

Unsafe systems trap energy into:
- Anxiety
- Rage
- Dissociation
- Shame
- Collapse
- Addiction
- Compulsion
- Ideology
- Violence

These are not moral failures.  
They are **field instabilities**.

---

## 4. Coherence

Coherence is the state where:
- Perception aligns with reality  
- Emotion aligns with truth  
- Action aligns with values  

A coherent system wastes almost no energy.

A collapsed system wastes nearly all of it.

Coherence is the physical signature of safety.

---

## 5. Phase States

All human systems exist in one of four primary EP phases:

1. **Safe & Coherent**  
2. **Safe but Activated**  
3. **Threatened**  
4. **Collapsed**

These phases apply to:
- Brains
- Families
- Corporations
- Nations
- Online communities
- AI-human systems

No system can skip these laws.

---

## 6. Why This Matters

When people fight, it is not because they are bad.  
When societies fracture, it is not because they are stupid.  
When systems become cruel, it is not because they are evil.

It is because their **safety field collapsed**.

EP gives us a way to:
- Measure this
- Predict this
- Repair this
- Design against this
