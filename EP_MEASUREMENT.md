# Emotional Physics — Measurement  
**EP by ACL**

Emotional Physics is observable.

Safety, threat, and coherence can be measured across nervous systems, relationships, organizations, and societies.

This document defines how EP states are detected.

---

## 1. The Measurement Principle

You cannot heal what you cannot detect.

In EP, safety and threat are measured by:
- Physiological signals  
- Behavioral signals  
- Cognitive signals  
- Social signals  

These signals always change before people do.

---

## 2. Physiological Indicators

A system is moving toward safety when:
- Breathing slows and deepens  
- Heart rate variability increases  
- Muscles relax  
- Sleep improves  
- Digestion improves  

A system is moving toward threat when:
- Breath becomes shallow  
- Heart rate spikes or flattens  
- Muscles tense  
- Sleep fragments  
- Pain increases  

These are field-level indicators.

---

## 3. Behavioral Indicators

Safety produces:
- Curiosity  
- Play  
- Cooperation  
- Learning  
- Creativity  

Threat produces:
- Aggression  
- Withdrawal  
- Addiction  
- Rigidity  
- Control  

Behavior reveals the safety field.

---

## 4. Cognitive Indicators

Safety produces:
- Flexible thinking  
- Nuance  
- Humor  
- Self-reflection  

Threat produces:
- Black-and-white thinking  
- Catastrophizing  
- Blame  
- Ideology  

The mind narrows when safety collapses.

---

## 5. Social Indicators

Safe systems show:
- Trust  
- Transparency  
- Repair  
- Mutual aid  

Threatened systems show:
- Secrecy  
- Punishment  
- Scapegoating  
- Fear-based compliance  

Culture reflects the field.

---

## 6. Long-Term Markers

Chronic threat produces:
- Trauma  
- Illness  
- Violence  
- Authoritarianism  
- Burnout  

Chronic safety produces:
- Innovation  
- Healing  
- Prosperity  
- Cooperation  
- Meaning  

---

## 7. Why This Works

These signals are cross-cultural and biological.

They cannot be faked for long.

Safety leaves a signature in the body.
